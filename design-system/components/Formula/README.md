# Formula

The brand's sentence: **Свобода = …**. Freedom equals the elements a woman chooses. Terms are chips holding a glyph and the element's word; the operators `=` and `+` are set in Unbounded in the violet → magenta gradient.

- `ff-formula` (18px words), `ff-formula--sm` (14px), and `ff-formula--icons`, glyphs only with no words, which is used for the **band** between sections (a full-width night strip with every element). Icon-only terms carry the word in `aria-label`.
- Use the word version as a caption under the periodic block and in the formula builder's result.
- Keep it short: Свобода plus two or three terms. The band is the only place all eight appear.
- Copy pattern: "Для когось свобода — це дохід. Для когось — старт і воля."
